2. Problem Statement

70% of Gen Z in Latin America lack consistent savings habits. Traditional banks offer boring, complex interfaces that don't resonate with young users. Existing fintech apps (Nubank, Mercado Pago) focus on payments and credit, not savings behavior change. Young adults want to save but lack motivation, accountability, and tools that feel native to their digital-first, social-media-driven lifestyle.

3. Proposed Solution

A mobile-first savings app with gamification mechanics inspired by Duolingo: daily savings streaks, challenges (e.g., "No Uber for a week"), social savings groups with friends, leaderboards, and cashback rewards from partner brands. Users link their bank account, set goals, and the app creates micro-savings automations while making the process feel like a game rather than a chore.
